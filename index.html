From your journey to Lightroom

  1. Export Timeline data from Google Maps on your phone, or export Apple Health and select its ZIP archive with workout routes.
  2. Add one or more location files. Select a day and inspect the recorded, estimated and missing sections.
  3. Export GPX. In Lightroom Classic, open Map → Tracklog → Load Tracklog, then select photos and use Auto-Tag.

Use Lightroom’s Tracklog Time Offset if your camera clock differs. GeoConversion keeps all output timestamps in UTC. No photographs are uploaded.

Inputs are limited to 128 MB per file and one million combined samples. Apple Health ZIP processing extracts only workout GPX routes; health XML is skipped. CSV timestamps must contain a timezone. KML requires gx:Track timestamps or timed point placemarks.
